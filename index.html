What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which attackers impersonate trusted organizations or individuals to steal sensitive information such as passwords, banking credentials, OTPs, and personal information.

Attackers commonly use emails, text messages, fake websites, and social engineering techniques to deceive victims.

Phishing attacks remain one of the most successful forms of cybercrime because they target human behavior rather than technical vulnerabilities.

Types of Phishing Attacks

Email Phishing

Fraudulent emails pretending to be legitimate organizations.

Spear Phishing

Targeted attacks aimed at specific individuals or companies.

Whaling

Attacks focused on executives and high-profile employees.

Smishing

SMS-based phishing attacks designed to steal information.

Vishing

Voice phishing conducted through phone calls.

Recognizing Phishing Emails

Warning Signs

  • Unknown sender address.
  • Urgent or threatening language.
  • Spelling and grammar mistakes.
  • Suspicious links.
  • Unexpected attachments.
  • Requests for passwords or OTPs.

Example Email

From: support@amaz0n-security.com

Your account has been suspended. Click immediately to verify your account.

Red Flags: Fake domain, urgency, suspicious request.

Identifying Fake Websites

Legitimate

amazon.com

Fake

amaz0n-login.com

  • Check the URL carefully.
  • Look for HTTPS.
  • Watch for spelling mistakes.
  • Avoid suspicious popups.
  • Verify the domain name.

Social Engineering Tactics

Fear

Your account will be locked.

Urgency

Act immediately.

Authority

Fake bank or company representatives.

Curiosity

You received a secret document.

How to Stay Safe

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication.
  • Verify email addresses.
  • Never share OTPs.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links.
  • Report phishing attempts.

Real World Examples

Banking Scam

Fake banking emails asking users to verify accounts.

Delivery Scam

Messages claiming package delivery failure.

Job Scam

Fake recruitment offers requesting money.
